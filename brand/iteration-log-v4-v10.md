# Logo iteration log · v4 to v10

Every version is preserved in `concepts/` and rendered under identical visual
conditions in `review/screenshots/`. The aggregate comparison is
`review/screenshots/logo-system-all-versions.png`.

## V4 · rejected

Improved local drawing quality, but remained five unrelated pictograms. Family
recognition depended on color and stroke. Goshtoso and Manja lost meaning at
16 px; reverse previews were invalid CSS filters.

## V5 · useful direction, not selected

Introduced one shared `storm hood` outline. Family recognition became
immediate, and native reverse plus true 16 px tests passed structurally. The
master silhouette read as an arch, tunnel, or headphones rather than a dark
cloud.

## V6 · rejected

Changed the hood into a solid high-contrast cell. Small-size reproduction
improved, but the silhouette read as a tombstone, helmet, or ghost.

## V7 · viable baseline

Replaced the cell with a solid curved cloud. This restored the Arai Hû meaning
and kept product distinctions at 16 px. The first monochrome transformation was
wrong for a solid container, causing colored internals to merge into the cloud;
the history renderer now converts those internals into paper cutouts.

## V8 · rejected after context review

Adds a small charged notch to the cloud's lower contour. Compared with v7, the
family remains immediately cloud-derived while gaining a repeatable proprietary
detail. Product nuclei remain minimal:

- Arai Hû: charged diagonal;
- Goshtoso: four composable modules;
- Manja: open API publication and endpoint;
- Pajé: five-checkpoint durable route;
- Xisnove: three external health states.

V8 passed normalized color, blind monochrome, true 16 px, outlined wordmark,
native reverse, favicon, and site-context checks. It was rejected because every
product inherited the organization cloud.

## V9 · rejected

Varied the shared cloud silhouettes to reduce container repetition. Product
signs became less identical, but still violated the final rule: cloud belongs
only to Arai Hû.

## V10 · selected

Keeps the accepted charged cloud only for Arai Hû. Product signs are standalone
and semantic: component panels, open API publication, durable route, and
monitoring signal. Family relation comes from one grid, rounded 14-unit stroke,
midnight/cobalt structure, and one lime active state.

V10 passed normalized color, blind monochrome, true 16 px, outlined wordmark,
native reverse, favicon, current-site comparison, canonical byte equality, and
SVG structure/accessibility/palette gates.
