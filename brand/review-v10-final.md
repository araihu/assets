## Accepted direction

Only the Arai Hû organization mark uses a cloud. Each product owns a standalone
symbol tied to its purpose. Family resemblance comes from geometry, stroke,
corners, palette, and one lime active state—not a repeated container.

- Arai Hû: charged cloud;
- Goshtoso: overlapping component panels and live module;
- Manja: open API publication and endpoint;
- Pajé: continuous durable route and checkpoints;
- Xisnove: three-state monitoring signal and external probe.

## Visual gates

- identical rendering conditions exist for every ready version V2–V10;
- color, blind monochrome, native reverse, outlined lockup, favicon, and true
  16 px views are preserved;
- product signs remain distinct without labels;
- no product reuses the Arai Hû cloud path or cloud language;
- all canonical SVGs are self-contained, accessible, and font-independent;
- Arai Hû, Goshtoso, Manja, and Pajé were compared against live site captures;
- Xisnove was checked in a faithful monitoring-shell preview because its public
  hostname did not resolve during review.
